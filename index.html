Since we're on the subject of our buildings: Residences at One Eleven

A few blocks from tonight's route, at 111 Lyon St NW, is Michigan's first major office-to-residential conversion — 140 apartments (105 one-bedroom, 35 two-bedroom), 28 income-qualified, converted from 150,000 square feet of oversupplied office space. It opened this summer, sixty years to the day after the building's original 1966 dedication — city leaders have already pointed to it as a model for other downtown conversions to follow. Walking distance to Van Andel Arena, the Acrisure Amphitheater, and the city's major hospitals.

If you only remember three things

→

Downtown is genuinely walkable.

Ottawa Ave, Monroe Center, Fulton St, Bridge St NW. That's basically the whole route above, and you could do this entire week on foot without moving your car once.

→

You can live here, and you can work here.

Residents at 111 Lyon already do. Ledyard's next. CWD's own buildings, like 38 Fulton, sit right inside the walk.

→

This isn't just a Tech Week thing.

It's fun to be down here, and it happens all year round, not just this one week in September.
